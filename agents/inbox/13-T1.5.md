## Requirements

### `segmenter.py` — `cues_from_transcript(transcript, *, max_chars=42, max_lines=2, max_dur=7.0, min_dur=1.0, min_gap=0.08) -> list[Cue]`
`Cue(index (1-based), start, end, lines: list[str], speaker: str | None, word_span: tuple[int, int])` (`word_span` = first/last global word index,
used later by translation). Algorithm over the flat word list (all segments, in time order):
1. **Hard breaks** (always end the current cue before the word): speaker change; a pause > 1.0 s between words; the cue would exceed
   `max_dur` or `max_lines × max_chars` characters (measured on `join_words` text).
2. **Preferred breaks** — once the cue is ≥ `min_dur` and ≥ 15 chars, end it after a word ending in sentence punctuation (`. ? ! …` and
   CJK `。！？`); failing that, near the limit, after `, ; : 、，`.
3. Never split inside a word; never mix speakers in one cue.
4. **Lines**: if the cue text > `max_chars`, split into 2 lines at the word boundary that best balances line lengths with both ≤ `max_chars`
   (prefer breaking after punctuation when the imbalance is within 8 chars). A single word longer than `max_chars` stays on its own line.
5. **Timing**: start = first word start, end = last word end; extend `end` to reach `min_dur` only as far as `next.start − min_gap`; if two cues
   would be closer than `min_gap`, pull the earlier end back; never overlap; never > `max_dur` (split instead).
6. Text uses `join_words` (no space artefacts). CJK text without spaces counts characters as-is.

### `formats.py`
- `to_srt(cues) -> str`: `1\n00:00:01,000 --> 00:00:03,500\nline1\nline2\n\n` …; times `HH:MM:SS,mmm`, rounded to ms, hours may exceed 99 → still 2+ digits.
- `to_vtt(cues, *, voice_tags=True) -> str`: `WEBVTT\n\n` header; `HH:MM:SS.mmm`; escape `&`→`&amp;`, `<`→`&lt;`, `>`→`&gt;` in text; with
  `voice_tags` and a known speaker, first line prefixed `<v S1>`.
- Both end with a single trailing newline; UTF-8; `\n` line endings.
